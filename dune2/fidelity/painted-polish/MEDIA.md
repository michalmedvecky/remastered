# Painted object preview integration

`createPaintedMedia()` preloads the complete new illustration, part and cleanplate bank before returning. `draw(ctx, frame, {scene, occluders})` identifies the actual native184×112 preview at `(128,48)` using exact native palette-index witnesses. It draws at the physical4× canvas coordinates and returns `drawn,nativeRect,resource,frame,status,motionStatus`; `diagnostics()` records coverage and unknown/ambiguous observations.

All42 native resources and264 stored display phases are covered by33 global indexed witnesses. The264 records contain210 distinct pictures, with duplicate-frame holds retained. Factory previews use only actual native frame0. Mentat animations follow the observed native phase; no separate clock advances them. Every original frame's SHA256 and identification is tested, and every phase has a supported new-art composition plan.

The compositor uses36 newly painted whole bases, nine matched transparent dish/marshal/guard/transport parts and six matched inpainted cleanplates generated from the new paintings. Remaining lamp, fan, intake, dust, plume, welding and flame motion uses small purpose-authored semantic shapes or geometry transforms of the new painted fan disc. Original RGB is never used to shade a new picture. Cleanplates blend over one native pixel at their edges; original geometry/phase evidence does not become a low resolution image overlay.

Six additional painted whole bases cover `FARTR.WSA`, `FHARK.WSA`, `FORDOS.WSA`, `FREMEN.WSA`, `WORM.WSA` and `SARDUKAR.WSA`. Their103 native phases use the complete painted base plus97 bounded replacement patches. The three house illustrations preserve fixed crests while their painted homeworlds rotate through30 observed phases each. FREMEN retains the two equipment-lamp states, WORM is static, and SARDUKAR retains ten independently observed lamp-intensity combinations. Patches replace only their declared local rectangle on a freshly restored new base; no prior phase accumulates. The original36 bases, parts, cleanplates and161 semantic plans remain unchanged.

Observed UI glyph/sprite/border ink and an optional exact cursor mask exclude only known covered witness pixels. A mismatch returns unknown; different candidate images return ambiguous. The compositor does not select the nearest phase or paint over uncertain observations. Native alias frames that are pixel-identical share one visual phase.

The new whole paintings require explicit semantic anchor calibration. Notable bindings are the BARRAC fan centre `(140,72)`, HEADQRTS dish rectangle `(149,42,24,17)`, CARRYALL marshal foot near `(159,101)`, and the PALACE guards aligned with their new painted counterparts. STARPORT's transport retains original observed horizontal phase positions, while its new apron path is at native y94–99, below the new foreground barrels; its original illustrative path was y70–75. These are presentation-layout differences and do not affect simulation or input. The tiny pulley/marker/conveyor semantics remain the explicitly recorded uncertainties in the motion inventory. Newly painted poses are coherent approximations, not a claim of original pixel identity.
